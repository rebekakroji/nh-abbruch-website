# DESIGN SYSTEM — NH ABBRUCH & RENOVIERUNG

## 1. Brand Identity

Company name: NH Abbruch & Renovierung
Industry: Demolition, dismantling, renovation preparation
Location: Freising, Germany
Service area: Freising + approximately 100 km

The website must communicate professionalism, reliability, precision, strength, and trust.

Create a premium German construction company aesthetic. The design should feel modern, confident, clean, and established — not flashy, generic, or overly decorative.

The visual identity must be consistent across the entire website.

## 2. Logo Guidelines

Use the official NH Abbruch & Renovierung logo provided by the client.

* Preserve the original logo design and proportions.
* Do not change the company name.
* The exact wording is "NH Abbruch & Renovierung".
* Do not replace "Renovierung" with "Demontage".
* Use the logo in the header and footer.
* Keep the logo visually clear and appropriately sized.
* Do not add unnecessary effects, shadows, or decorations to the logo.

## 3. Color Palette

Use a restrained, professional construction-inspired palette.

* Primary Charcoal: #171C24
* Deep Navy: #202936
* Construction Orange: #F36B16
* Warm White: #F7F7F5
* Pure White: #FFFFFF
* Concrete Grey: #D9DDE1
* Body Text Grey: #626A73

Color usage:

* Charcoal and deep navy for the header, footer, and strong visual sections.
* White and warm white for clean content areas.
* Construction orange for primary CTA buttons, highlights, and small accents.
* Grey for secondary text, borders, and subtle backgrounds.

Avoid excessive orange. Use it strategically to guide attention.

## 4. Typography

Use a modern, bold, highly readable sans-serif typeface.

Recommended fonts:

* Headings: Montserrat or Manrope
* Body text: Inter

Typography style:

* Bold, confident headings.
* Clean and readable body text.
* Strong contrast between headings and paragraphs.
* Consistent font weights and line heights.
* Avoid decorative, handwritten, or overly futuristic fonts.

Use responsive font sizes with CSS clamp() where appropriate.

## 5. Layout & Spacing

Use a structured, spacious, premium layout.

* Center content within a consistent max-width container.
* Use generous but balanced vertical spacing.
* Maintain consistent horizontal alignment.
* Use a clear grid system for services and gallery sections.
* Avoid overly narrow content areas.
* Avoid excessive empty space.
* Keep sections visually connected and balanced.

Use consistent spacing variables rather than arbitrary values.

## 6. Header & Navigation

Create a professional, clean navigation bar.

* Logo aligned to the left.
* Navigation links aligned to the right.
* A clearly visible orange CTA button: "Kostenloses Angebot anfragen".
* Use a charcoal or white header depending on the overall composition.
* Keep navigation simple and readable.
* Use subtle hover states.
* Use a clean mobile hamburger menu.
* Ensure the header works well on all screen sizes.

## 7. Hero Section

The hero section is one of the most important parts of the website.

It should immediately communicate the company's services and create a strong first impression.

* Use one of the real company photos as the hero image.
* Prefer a wide, visually strong construction or renovation photo.
* Use a dark overlay only when necessary for text readability.
* Use a bold headline with a clear visual hierarchy.
* Keep the content concise and impactful.
* Include a primary orange CTA.
* Include a secondary, less prominent CTA.
* Maintain excellent text contrast.

The hero should feel premium, bold, and authentic — not like a generic template.

## 8. Services Section

Present the company's services in a clean, organized grid.

* Use consistent card dimensions and spacing.
* Use simple, professional icons where appropriate.
* Keep descriptions short and easy to scan.
* Use subtle borders or background contrast.
* Avoid excessive rounded corners.
* Avoid heavy shadows and unnecessary decoration.
* Use orange accents sparingly.
* Make the layout responsive.

Cards should feel professional and functional rather than playful.

## 9. About Section

Create a trustworthy and authentic presentation of the company.

* Use a balanced text-and-image layout.
* Use real company photography when suitable.
* Maintain a strong heading hierarchy.
* Highlight reliability, precision, and customer-oriented service.
* Avoid generic stock-style illustrations.
* Do not invent company history, certifications, or years of experience.

## 10. References & Image Gallery

Use the real company photos provided by the client.

* Create a professional, visually consistent gallery.
* Use a responsive grid.
* Maintain consistent image ratios where appropriate.
* Use object-fit: cover when suitable.
* Avoid stretching or distorting images.
* Use subtle hover effects.
* Avoid excessive animations.
* Do not invent project details or testimonials.

The gallery should showcase the actual work in a clean and credible way.

## 11. Contact Section

The contact section should be clear, accessible, and conversion-focused.

* Use a strong, direct heading.
* Make email and WhatsApp easy to find.
* Use a prominent orange contact CTA.
* Keep the contact form simple and visually clean.
* Use clear labels and accessible form controls.
* Use sufficient spacing between form fields.
* Make the section responsive and easy to use on mobile.

## 12. Buttons & Interactions

Primary buttons:

* Construction orange background.
* Dark or white text with accessible contrast.
* Strong, readable font weight.
* Moderate corner radius.
* Comfortable padding.
* Subtle hover transition.

Secondary buttons:

* Outline or neutral background.
* Clear visual distinction from primary CTAs.

All interactive elements must have visible hover and keyboard-focus states.

Avoid exaggerated animations, bouncing effects, or flashy transitions.

## 13. Photography Guidelines

Real company photography is a key part of the brand.

* Prioritize the client's uploaded photos.
* Use the most visually suitable photo in the hero.
* Match photos to relevant services based on their actual content.
* Preserve image quality and proportions.
* Use professional cropping.
* Avoid repeating the same image unnecessarily.
* Do not use unrelated construction stock photos if real photos are available.
* Do not invent project information.

## 14. Responsive Design

The website must work beautifully on:

* Desktop
* Laptop
* Tablet
* Mobile

Requirements:

* Mobile-first responsive behavior.
* No horizontal overflow.
* Responsive image sizing.
* Readable typography on small screens.
* Mobile-friendly buttons and forms.
* Properly adapted navigation.
* Consistent spacing at all breakpoints.

Do not simply shrink the desktop layout. Adapt the composition to each screen size.

## 15. Accessibility

* Maintain accessible color contrast.
* Use semantic HTML.
* Provide meaningful image alt text.
* Ensure keyboard navigation.
* Provide visible focus states.
* Use accessible form labels.
* Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## 16. Final Design Direction

The final website should look like a premium, modern German demolition and renovation company website.

The design should be:

* Professional
* Bold
* Minimal
* Trustworthy
* Modern
* Clean
* Visually consistent

Avoid:

* Generic AI website aesthetics
* Excessive gradients
* Too many rounded cards
* Excessive animations
* Unnecessary decorative elements
* Cluttered layouts
* Fake statistics or testimonials

Every design decision should support the company's identity and make it easy for visitors to understand the services and request a quote.

This design system is the source of truth for the entire NH Abbruch & Renovierung website.
